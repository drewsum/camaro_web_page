# Flywheel

Built by `build_flywheel.py`, after the crank. Collection `BBC_Flywheel`; all four parts are parented to `Crankshaft`, so they turn with `crank_deg`. Results are in `flywheel_info.json`.

Drew's car is a manual 4-speed. Album photos 25, 35 and 36 show a steel flywheel with a 168-tooth ring gear under an 11" clutch.

Balance: drawn neutral (internally balanced) [est]. No counterweight is visible in the photos, and the 540's balance is not on the build sheet.

## Checks

- Every 10° of crank: no contact with the block, crank, rear cap and studs, the rear rods, or between the parts.
- The front face sits .40" behind the block's rear face.
- Weight from the mesh volume (steel): flywheel 27.6 lb, ring gear 2.2 lb, about 30 lb in all. Listed 168-tooth steel flywheels are 28-36 lb.

## Not modelled

- Clutch, pressure plate, bellhousing and the engine (block) plate.
- Pressure-plate dowels.
- The crank's register spigot: the 2.485" centre hole shows the plain crank flange.
